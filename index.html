    📘 User Manual

    Introduction & Purpose: Zoo Adventure is an interactive multimedia courseware designed for primary school pupils learning English. It allows learners to explore 5 wild animals, master pronunciations, watch educational videos, play sound-based guess games, complete matching activities, and test knowledge via quizzes.

    Navigation & Controls

    • Top Navigation Bar: Jump directly between any of the 5 learning sections.
    • Pronunciation: Click the 🔊 speaker button in Section 1 to hear native English animal name pronunciations.
    • Watch & Learn: Click YouTube controls to play/pause the animal video.
    • Sound Guessing: Click ▶ PLAY SOUND, listen to synthesized animal audio, and pick the correct animal card.
    • Matching: Click an animal card, then click its matching description text. Press "Check Answers" when complete.
    • Quiz: Select one of three choices per question for immediate feedback and final scores.

    ⚖ Ethical Planning & Development

    This multimedia courseware complies with educational ethics and digital learning standards:

    • Age-Appropriate Content: Text, vocabulary, and visuals are tailored specifically for primary school ESL learners.
    • Copyright & Attribution: Embedded media relies on open YouTube embedding and royalty-free educational graphics.
    • Privacy Protection: No personal student data, login credentials, or tracking cookies are collected or stored.
    • Accessibility: High-contrast typography and clear text feedback are provided alongside audio cues.
